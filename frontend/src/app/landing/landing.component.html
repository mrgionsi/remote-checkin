FAQ

Answers that keep your rollout on track.

How fast can we launch?

Most customers go live in under two weeks: connect PMS, add email templates, invite staff. We provide migration templates and proven onboarding playbooks.

What compliance coverage is included?

We ship with configurable legal forms for multiple regions. Export reports directly for authorities with the same encryption standards proven across our remote check-in workflows.

Can we extend the platform?

Yes. Use our REST and webhook APIs to connect marketing automation, housekeeping platforms, or custom guest apps. SDKs ship with authentication helpers and sample bots.

Do you support mobile-first experiences?

Absolutely. Guests can complete the journey on any device, with real-time validation optimised for cameras and offline capture queues for limited connectivity.
